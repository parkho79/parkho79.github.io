My Calendar

Calendar & planner with public holidays, adjustable large text and month widget

Google Play

A clean, easy-to-read calendar, planner and calendar widget that shows your schedule together with public holidays. Dates and events can be shown in large text, and the events already on your phone appear automatically.

Features

Public holidays

  • United States and many more countries
  • Show holidays from several countries together
  • Holiday data updated automatically

Large, easy-to-read text

  • Set date and event text sizes separately
  • See every change in a live preview

Lunar dates

  • Lunar date beside or under each day
  • Korean, Chinese and Vietnamese lunar calendars

Four views

  • Year, month, week and day views
  • Multi-day events drawn as continuous bars

Home screen widget

  • Month widget with events and holidays
  • Change months or jump to today on the widget

Events and reminders

  • All-day and repeating events
  • Drag to move, reminders and search
