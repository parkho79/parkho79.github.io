Simple Lunar Calendar

Korean lunar calendar with holidays, 24 solar terms, lunar reminders and widget

Google PlayONE store

A clean calendar that shows the Korean lunar date under every day, together with public holidays and the 24 solar terms. No sign-up or login needed, and lunar dates and solar terms work offline.

Features

Lunar dates and holidays

  • The lunar date on every day, including leap months
  • Korean public and substitute holidays
  • Tap a day to see its lunar date and memos

Solar terms and traditions

  • All 24 solar terms, Hansik and the three Bok days
  • Traditional days such as Daeboreum, Dano and Chilseok
  • Stems and branches, lucky moving days

Memos and reminders

  • Repeat every year or month by the lunar date
  • Weekly, monthly, yearly or custom repeats
  • Several reminders per memo, 6 colors

Home screen widget

  • 4x2, 4x3, 4x4 and 5x5 month widgets
  • Move between months on the widget
  • Adjust transparency and text size

Make it yours

  • Basic, Wall and Long life calendar themes
  • Navy, green, red or wallpaper colors, light and dark
  • Adjustable date, lunar and memo text sizes

Backup and restore

  • Back up memos to a file and restore them on a new phone
